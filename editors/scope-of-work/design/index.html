Scope of Work — three concepts to play with

Each is a self-contained mockup with realistic dummy data. Budgets and progress are computed live with the same rules as the reducers. Click, edit, drag; nothing is saved. The full rationale is in README.md.

CONCEPT A · recommended shell

Ledger

Outline rail · document canvas · inspector. The plan reads top-to-bottom like a proposal; every field edits in place; the inspector holds the heavy controls (progress, placement, quote). Overview carries the delivery spine and the plan matrix.

Best for: day-to-day editing with the hierarchy always visible
CONCEPT B

Board

The projects × milestones matrix as the home screen. Drag a card to reschedule (column) or refund (row). Tabs for a filterable table with bulk edit, a budget ledger, and the team. Editing happens in a slide-over drawer.

Best for: overview and re-planning; power users
CONCEPT C

Flow

Starts from an empty document. Six guided steps with completeness meters, a live summary rail, and a review screen that is exactly what a reviewer sees. Submit unlocks when the checklist is green.

Best for: first-time population; proposal authors
